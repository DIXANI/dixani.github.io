Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DIXANI handles information, uses analytics technologies and may use advertising services when you visit the website or use its tools.

DIXANI tools are designed to process the information you enter locally in your browser where possible. Please avoid entering confidential, sensitive or personally identifiable information into calculators or tools unless a tool specifically states that such information is required.

Information You Provide to DIXANI Tools

DIXANI provides online calculators, converters, generators and other practical tools. Unless a particular tool explicitly states otherwise, information entered into these tools is processed locally within your web browser and is not intentionally submitted to or stored by DIXANI.

You are responsible for deciding what information you enter into a DIXANI tool. We recommend that you do not enter confidential, sensitive or personally identifiable information when it is not necessary to use the tool.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit DIXANI, certain technical information may be collected automatically by analytics, hosting or other website services. This may include information such as your browser type, device type, approximate geographic region, referring pages, pages visited, IP address and general interaction with the website.

This information may be used to understand how visitors use DIXANI, maintain the website, identify technical problems and improve the tools and content available on the site.

Google Analytics

DIXANI uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and general visitor activity. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about how visitors interact with the website.

The information provided by analytics helps DIXANI understand which pages and tools are useful, how visitors navigate the website and where the website can be improved.

Google's collection and use of information is governed by its own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies

Cookies are small data files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. DIXANI and third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as analytics, website functionality, advertising, fraud prevention and measurement.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website or advertising features.

Advertising and Google AdSense

DIXANI may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other approved advertising partners.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to DIXANI or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visits to DIXANI and/or other websites on the Internet.

Depending on applicable settings, consent choices and local requirements, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Personalized Advertising Choices

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising from Google through Google Ads Settings .

Users may also learn more about choices relating to interest-based advertising through AboutAds .

Third-party advertising vendors or ad networks may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies in connection with advertisements displayed on DIXANI.

DIXANI does not directly control cookies or technologies used by independent third-party services. Their use of information is subject to their respective privacy policies and settings.
