PRACTICAL LEARNING

Why decision simulations are useful

Warehouse performance is affected by connected decisions. Improving one KPI without considering the others can create new risks. This challenge encourages balanced decisions rather than focusing only on speed or cost.

Key principle

The strongest operational decision normally protects people, inventory and customer commitments while maintaining controlled material flow.
