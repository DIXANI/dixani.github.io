GOOD INVENTORY PRACTICE

Why the sheet does not show system quantity

The DIXANI Stock Count Sheet is primarily designed as a physical or blind-count document.

Showing the expected system quantity to the person performing the count can influence the result. Recording the physical quantity independently helps create a cleaner count record.

After counting, the physical quantity can be compared with the inventory system to identify shortages, excess stock and other discrepancies.

FAQ

Stock Count Sheet — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a stock count sheet?

A stock count sheet is a document used to record the quantity of inventory physically found during an inventory count.

Can I edit the Excel stock count template?

Yes. The Excel version can be edited and adapted to suit your warehouse, store or business requirements.

Can I use this template for cycle counting?

Yes. The template can be used for full inventory counts, cycle counts, spot checks and blind counts.

Why is there a recount quantity column?

The recount column allows a second physical count to be recorded when the original quantity requires verification.

How do I calculate the stock variance after counting?

Compare the physical quantity with the system quantity. You can use the free DIXANI Inventory Variance Report Generator to calculate shortages, excess quantities and variance values.
