FAQ

Inventory Templates — Frequently Asked Questions

Are these inventory templates free?

Yes. DIXANI inventory templates are available as free downloads with no account required.

Can I edit the Excel inventory templates?

Yes. Excel versions can be edited and adapted to match your products, warehouse locations and inventory procedures.

Which template should I use for a physical stock count?

Use the Stock Count Sheet to record physical quantities by SKU and location. You can then use the Stock Variance Report to compare the count against system inventory.

What is the difference between a stock count sheet and a stock variance report?

A stock count sheet records physical inventory quantities. A stock variance report compares those quantities with system stock to identify shortages or excess inventory.

Can these templates be used without an ERP system?

Yes. They are designed for practical inventory control where a full ERP or warehouse-management system may not be available or where supporting Excel and paper records are still required.
