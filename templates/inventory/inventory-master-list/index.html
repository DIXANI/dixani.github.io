FAQ

Inventory Master List — Frequently Asked Questions

What is an inventory master list?

An inventory master list is a central record containing the core information used to identify and manage each inventory item.

What is an SKU?

SKU means Stock Keeping Unit. It is a unique internal code used by a business to identify and manage a product or inventory item.

Can I use the template for e-commerce products?

Yes. The template can be used for many small-business and e-commerce inventory operations that need a structured SKU and product master.

Does the template detect duplicate SKUs?

Yes. Duplicate SKU codes are highlighted to help identify potential master-data errors.

Does it calculate product profit?

Yes. Enter unit cost and selling price and the workbook calculates gross profit and gross margin percentage.

What should I do with discontinued products?

Rather than deleting historical item information or reusing the SKU for another product, you can mark the item as discontinued in the Status field.
