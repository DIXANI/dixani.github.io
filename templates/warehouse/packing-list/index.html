FAQ

Packing List — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a warehouse packing list?

A warehouse packing list records the products, quantities and packages prepared for an order or outbound shipment.

What is the difference between a picking list and a packing list?

A picking list guides warehouse staff while collecting products from storage locations. A packing list records the items and quantities actually packed for shipment.

Does the Excel template calculate CBM?

Yes. Enter package length, width and height in centimetres and the workbook automatically calculates cubic metres.

Can I record carton numbers?

Yes. Carton / Package No. and Units / Package fields are included for shipment identification.

Can I record shipment weights?

Yes. The template includes both Net Weight and Gross Weight fields together with automatic totals.

Can I use this for e-commerce orders?

Yes. It can be used for warehouse shipments, online orders, small-business fulfillment and other packing operations.

Can I print the packing list?

Yes. A separate landscape A4 PDF is included for manual packing and dispatch records.
