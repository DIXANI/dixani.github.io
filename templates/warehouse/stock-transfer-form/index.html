What is a Stock Transfer Form?

A Stock Transfer Form documents inventory moved from one warehouse, branch, bin or stock location to another and provides a record of the quantities dispatched and received.

✓

Record preparation, dispatch, receipt and approval of each stock transfer.

FAQ

Stock Transfer Form — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Stock Transfer Form?

A Stock Transfer Form records inventory moved between warehouses, branches, bins or other stock locations.

What is the difference between transferred and received quantity?

Transferred quantity is the quantity dispatched from the source location. Received quantity is the quantity physically confirmed at the destination.

How does the Excel template identify shortages?

The workbook compares Received Qty with Transferred Qty. If the received quantity is lower, the transfer is identified as a Short Receipt.

Can I use the template for bin-to-bin transfers?

Yes. The template includes From Location / Bin and To Location / Bin fields and can be used for internal warehouse location transfers.

Can I record batch numbers?

Yes. Batch / Lot information and stock condition can be recorded for each transferred item.

Can I print the Stock Transfer Form?

Yes. The downloadable PDF is designed as a landscape A4 form for manual transfer and receiving records.
