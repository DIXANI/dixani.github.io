WAREHOUSE STOCK ISSUE

What is a Goods Issue Note?

A Goods Issue Note, commonly called a GIN, is used to document inventory released from a warehouse against an approved requirement or request.

✓

Control Stock Issues

Document which inventory was released and who received it.

✓

Compare Quantities

Compare requested quantities against the quantities actually issued.

✓

Track Locations

Record the source bin or warehouse location from which stock was picked.

✓

Document Authorization

Maintain issued, received, checked and approved sign-off records.

FAQ

Goods Issue Note — Frequently Asked Questions

What does GIN stand for?

GIN stands for Goods Issue Note. It documents inventory released from a warehouse or stock location.

What is the difference between a GRN and GIN?

A GRN records inventory received into the warehouse. A GIN records inventory issued out of the warehouse.

What happens if the full requested quantity cannot be issued?

Enter the actual issued quantity. The Excel workbook calculates the short quantity and identifies the item as PARTIAL when appropriate.

Can I record the warehouse bin location?

Yes. The template includes a Source Bin / Location field for each item.

Can batch or lot numbers be recorded?

Yes. A Batch / Lot field is included for inventory requiring traceability.

Can I print the Goods Issue Note?

Yes. A separate landscape A4 PDF version is provided for manual completion and warehouse sign-off.
