FAQ

Damage & Write-Off Form — Frequently Asked Questions

What is an inventory write-off?

An inventory write-off records stock that has lost some or all of its usable or recoverable value and is removed or adjusted from available inventory after appropriate review.

When should a Damage & Write-Off Form be used?

It can be used for damaged, expired, obsolete, contaminated, lost or quality-rejected inventory requiring formal review.

How is write-off value calculated?

The Excel template calculates Write-Off Value by multiplying Quantity Affected by Unit Cost.

Can I record batch and expiry details?

Yes. Batch / Lot and Expiry Date fields are included for inventory requiring traceability.

Does the form approve a write-off automatically?

No. The template is a documentation and control tool. Actual authorization should follow your organization's approval limits and procedures.

Can I record how damaged stock was disposed of?

Yes. The template includes disposal options such as return to supplier, recycle, destroy, repair or rework, hold for review and other methods.

Can I print the form?

Yes. A separate landscape A4 PDF is included for manual warehouse write-off records.
