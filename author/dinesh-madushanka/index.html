Founder of DIXANI · Warehouse & Inventory Operations Supervisor · 3PL / 4PL Logistics · Based in Doha, Qatar

He created DIXANI to turn that experience into free, practical calculators, templates and guides for the everyday inventory, warehouse, logistics and purchasing tasks he has spent his career doing by hand, in spreadsheets, or inside large warehouse systems.

Why DIXANI exists

The idea behind DIXANI comes from seeing how often everyday work requires a quick calculation, a structured record or a simple planning tool without the need for complicated software. Every DIXANI tool and guide is built around a task from real operational work — a stock count, a reorder decision, a container load, a variance investigation — and every formula is documented on its page so the result can be checked by hand.

What Dinesh writes about: inventory control, cycle counting and stock accuracy, safety stock and reorder points, EOQ, warehouse capacity and space, container and load planning, and practical warehouse processes from goods receiving to dispatch.
