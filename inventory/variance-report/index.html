Inventory Variance Report — Frequently Asked Questions

What is an inventory variance?

Inventory variance is the difference between the quantity recorded in an inventory or warehouse system and the quantity physically counted. A negative variance generally indicates a shortage, while a positive variance indicates excess stock.

How is inventory variance calculated?

DIXANI calculates quantity variance by subtracting system quantity from physical quantity. Variance percentage is calculated against the system quantity.

Can I import inventory from Excel?

Version 1 supports CSV files, which can be exported from Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets and most inventory management systems. Direct XLSX upload can be added in a future version.

Is my inventory data uploaded to DIXANI?

No. The calculations in this version happen directly inside your browser. Your inventory data is not sent to a DIXANI server.
