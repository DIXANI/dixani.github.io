Why Is Safety Stock Important?

Safety stock is additional inventory kept as a buffer against uncertainty in customer demand, supplier lead time or both.

Without sufficient safety stock, unexpected demand increases or delivery delays can cause stockouts. Too much safety stock, however, can increase storage, financing, expiry and obsolescence costs.

The Simple Method is useful when you know average and maximum usage and lead times. The Advanced Method is more suitable when you have historical demand variability data and want to calculate safety stock using a target service level.
