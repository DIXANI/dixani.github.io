A CONNECTED APPROACH

Inventory control is a continuous process

Good inventory control requires more than completing a stock count. Each stage should produce reliable information for the next decision. Use the workflow below as a practical starting point and adapt the frequency and controls to your operation.

01
📊
MEASURE

Understand inventory accuracy

Compare system and physical quantities, calculate accuracy KPIs and identify the financial exposure created by stock discrepancies.

  • Measure exact-match accuracy
  • Calculate quantity and value variance
  • Identify items requiring attention
Open Inventory Accuracy Analyzer →
02
🔎
PRIORITIZE

Focus on the inventory that matters most

Use ABC analysis together with item value, movement, criticality and previous discrepancies to direct control effort where it creates the greatest operational benefit.

  • Classify inventory by annual usage value
  • Recognize high-value and high-risk items
  • Support counting and replenishment priorities
Read the ABC Analysis Guide →
03
✅
COUNT

Complete controlled physical counts

Prepare the area, control transactions, verify locations and record quantities consistently before making any system adjustment.

  • Plan the count scope and responsibilities
  • Use blind counts where appropriate
  • Recount material differences independently
Read the Cycle Count Guide → Download the Stock Count Sheet →
04
🧭
INVESTIGATE

Find the cause before adjusting stock

Review recent transactions, locations, units of measure, receiving, picking and documentation to understand why the discrepancy occurred.

  • Check transaction history and timing
  • Search nearby and alternative locations
  • Review receiving, picking and conversion errors
Open the Variance Investigation Guide →
05
📝
CORRECT

Document and approve corrections

Record the verified physical quantity, explain the root cause and complete authorized inventory adjustments with a clear audit trail.

  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions
  • Record quantity and financial impact
  • Obtain the required approval
Open the Variance Report Generator → Download the Variance Template →
06
📅
MONITOR

Build a repeatable counting schedule

Create a practical cycle-count schedule using ABC classes, count intervals and your team’s daily capacity. Record completed counts and review overdue work.

  • Set counting intervals by ABC class or individual item
  • Plan around working days and excluded dates
  • Track completion and export your schedule
Open Cycle Count Planner →
PRACTICAL CONTROL PRINCIPLES

A good count should lead to a better process

The objective is not simply to make the system match the physical quantity. The objective is to understand the difference, correct the record responsibly and prevent the same problem from recurring.

01Protect transaction integrity during counts
02Investigate before making adjustments
03Use risk to determine counting frequency
04Track recurring causes, not only quantities
