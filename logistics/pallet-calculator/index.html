Pallet Calculation Example

Suppose a carton measures 40 cm × 30 cm × 25 cm and the pallet measures 120 cm × 100 cm.

With carton rotation allowed, the simple-grid layout can place 9 cartons on each pallet layer.

9 cartons per layer × 5 layers = 45 cartons per pallet

If 200 cartons need to be palletized, the estimated requirement is:

200 ÷ 45 = 4.44 → 5 pallets required

In this example, 4 pallets would be fully loaded and the final pallet would contain 20 cartons.

What Does the Pallet Calculator Show?

The calculator estimates how cartons can be arranged on a pallet using a rectangular-grid layout.

It calculates cartons per layer, the number of layers that fit within the maximum loaded height, total cartons per pallet and the number of pallets required for the shipment.

The visual top view shows the selected carton formation, while the side view shows the estimated layer stack and loaded pallet height.
