Free Inventory Management Calculators

DIXANI inventory tools are designed to support common stock control, replenishment and inventory analysis tasks without requiring spreadsheet formulas or specialist software.

Inventory Replenishment & Stock Planning

Use the Reorder Point Calculator to estimate when stock should be reordered, and the Safety Stock Calculator to estimate additional buffer inventory for demand or supplier uncertainty.

The Economic Order Quantity Calculator can help estimate an order quantity that balances ordering and inventory holding costs.

Inventory Performance & Analysis

Use the Inventory Turnover Calculator to measure how frequently inventory moves during a reporting period.

The Days Inventory Outstanding Calculator converts inventory investment into an estimated number of inventory days and can compare current DIO against a target scenario.

Stock Accuracy & Variance Reporting

The Inventory Variance Report Generator compares system stock with physical inventory, identifies shortages and excess quantities, calculates variance value and creates a printable report.

All calculations run directly in your browser and the tools can be used without creating an account.
