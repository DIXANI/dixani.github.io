FAQ

Inventory Tools — Frequently Asked Questions

Are the DIXANI inventory calculators free?

Yes. The inventory tools are free to use and do not require an account.

Which calculator should I use to know when to reorder stock?

Use the Reorder Point Calculator to estimate the stock level that should trigger replenishment.

What is the difference between safety stock and reorder point?

Safety stock is additional buffer inventory. The reorder point is the stock level at which a new replenishment order should normally be placed.

What does inventory turnover measure?

Inventory turnover measures how frequently inventory is sold or consumed during a reporting period.

What does DIO mean?

DIO means Days Inventory Outstanding. It estimates the average number of days inventory remains held before being sold or used.

Can I use DIXANI inventory tools without an ERP system?

Yes. They are designed for straightforward inventory calculations where a full ERP or inventory-management system may not be available.
