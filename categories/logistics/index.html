FAQ

Warehouse & Logistics Tools — Frequently Asked Questions

Are the DIXANI logistics calculators free?

Yes. The logistics calculators are free to use and do not require an account.

What does CBM mean?

CBM means cubic meter. It is a common measurement used to describe shipment and cargo volume.

Can the Pallet Calculator calculate cartons per pallet?

Yes. It can estimate cartons per layer, layers per pallet, cartons per pallet and total pallets required.

What does the Container Loading Calculator estimate?

It estimates how many loose cartons may fit inside common shipping containers based on carton and container dimensions.

Can I estimate warehouse capacity?

Yes. The Warehouse Space & Storage Capacity Calculator can estimate warehouse area, pallet positions and storage capacity.

Are logistics calculator results exact?

No. The results are planning estimates. Actual loading and storage decisions should consider weight, safety, handling and operational restrictions.
