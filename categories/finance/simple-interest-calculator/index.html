UNDERSTANDING YOUR RESULTS

What your simple interest results mean

The calculation separates the original principal from the interest earned or charged, making it easier to understand how the rate and time period affect the final amount.

01

Principal Amount

The principal is the original amount borrowed, invested or saved. Simple interest is calculated from this original amount throughout the selected period.

02

Simple Interest

This is the total interest calculated from the principal, annual interest rate and time period. Previously accumulated interest is not added to the calculation base.

03

Total Amount

The total amount combines the original principal with the calculated simple interest. It shows the estimated final value at the end of the selected period.

04

Growth Percentage

The growth percentage shows the total simple interest as a percentage of the original principal over the full period entered into the calculator.

Quick example: At 5% simple interest per year, QAR 10,000 generates QAR 500 of interest each year. Over three years, the total simple interest is QAR 1,500.

EXAMPLE

Example simple interest calculation

Suppose you have a principal amount of QAR 10,000 at an annual simple interest rate of 5% for 3 years.

Principal: QAR 10,000 • Interest Rate: 5% • Time: 3 years

Simple Interest: QAR 10,000 × 5% × 3 = QAR 1,500

Total Amount: QAR 10,000 + QAR 1,500 = QAR 11,500

HOW IT WORKS

Simple interest formula

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal amount.

I = P × r × t

I = simple interest, P = principal, r = annual interest rate expressed as a decimal, and t = time in years.

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

The total amount is the original principal plus the calculated simple interest.

SIMPLE VS COMPOUND INTEREST

What's the difference?

Simple interest and compound interest calculate growth differently.

1

Simple Interest

Interest is calculated using the original principal throughout the entire period.

2

Compound Interest

Interest can earn additional interest because previously accumulated interest is added to the balance.

3

Time Period

For simple interest, the relationship between time and interest is linear when the rate remains constant.

FAQ

Simple Interest Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

What is simple interest?

Simple interest is interest calculated using only the original principal amount rather than adding previously earned interest to the calculation base.

How do you calculate simple interest?

Multiply the principal by the annual interest rate expressed as a decimal and by the time in years. The formula is I = P × r × t.

Can I enter the time in months?

Yes. Select Months and the calculator will convert the entered period into years for the simple interest calculation.

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is based on the original principal. Compound interest can also earn interest on previously accumulated interest.

Can simple interest be used for every loan?

No. Loan calculation methods vary. Some loans use amortization, reducing balances, fees or other methods. Use the calculation method specified by the lender or agreement.

Does this calculator include fees or taxes?

No. The calculation uses only the principal, interest rate and time period entered.

Is this financial advice?

No. This calculator is intended for general educational and planning purposes.
