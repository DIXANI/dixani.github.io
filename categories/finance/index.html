FAQ

Finance Calculators — Frequently Asked Questions

Are DIXANI finance calculators free?

Yes. The current DIXANI finance calculators are free to use without creating an account.

What finance calculators are currently available?

DIXANI currently provides calculators for break-even analysis, discounts, VAT, loan payments, compound interest and simple interest. Additional finance tools may be added over time.

What does a break-even calculator tell me?

It estimates how many units need to be sold for contribution to cover fixed costs.

Can the Discount Calculator calculate stacked discounts?

Yes. It can apply two discounts sequentially and calculate the effective total discount.

Are these calculators a replacement for accounting advice?

No. DIXANI calculators are intended for general planning and estimation rather than professional accounting, tax or financial advice.
