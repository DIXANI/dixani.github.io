Free Finance Calculators for Everyday Business Decisions

DIXANI finance calculators are designed to simplify common business calculations without requiring spreadsheet formulas or specialist financial software.

Break-Even Analysis

Use the Break-Even Calculator to estimate how many units must be sold before total contribution covers fixed costs.

The calculator also estimates break-even revenue, contribution per unit and contribution margin percentage. If you enter an expected sales volume, you can compare that volume against your calculated break-even point.

Pricing and Discount Calculations

Additional DIXANI finance tools will support common pricing calculations such as discounts, tax calculations and other practical financial planning tasks.

Simple Browser-Based Financial Tools

DIXANI calculators are designed for quick planning and estimation. Calculations run directly in your browser and can be used without creating an account.

Results are intended for general planning purposes and should not be considered financial, accounting or tax advice.
