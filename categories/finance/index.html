FAQ

Finance Tools — Frequently Asked Questions

Are DIXANI finance calculators free?

Yes. DIXANI finance calculators are intended to be free to use without requiring an account.

What is a break-even point?

The break-even point is the level of sales where total revenue equals total costs, meaning there is neither profit nor loss.

What does ROI mean?

ROI means Return on Investment. It compares the financial return from an investment with the amount invested.

Can I use these tools instead of accounting software?

No. These calculators are intended for simple planning and estimation rather than full accounting, bookkeeping or financial reporting.

Will more finance calculators be added?

Yes. Additional practical finance and business-planning calculators are planned for the DIXANI Finance category.
