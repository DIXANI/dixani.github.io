VAT FORMULAS

How VAT is calculated

Adding VAT and removing VAT require different formulas. Simply subtracting the VAT percentage from a gross amount does not correctly remove included VAT.

➕

Adding VAT

Multiply the net amount by the VAT rate to calculate the VAT amount.

VAT = Net × VAT Rate
🧾

Gross Amount

Add the calculated VAT amount to the original net amount.

Gross = Net + VAT
➖

Removing VAT

Divide the VAT-inclusive gross amount by one plus the VAT rate.

Net = Gross ÷ (1 + VAT Rate)

FAQ

VAT Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net amount by the VAT percentage to calculate the VAT amount, then add that VAT amount to the net value.

How do I remove VAT from a VAT-inclusive amount?

Divide the gross amount by one plus the VAT rate expressed as a decimal. The difference between the gross and calculated net amount is the included VAT.

Can I enter my own VAT rate?

Yes. The calculator uses the VAT percentage you enter, so it can be used with different VAT rates.

Why can't I simply subtract 5% from a VAT-inclusive price?

Because the VAT percentage is calculated from the net value, not from the VAT-inclusive gross value. Removing VAT therefore requires dividing the gross amount by one plus the VAT rate.

Does DIXANI determine which VAT rate I should use?

No. VAT rates and tax rules vary by jurisdiction and transaction. You should enter the rate that applies to your situation.
