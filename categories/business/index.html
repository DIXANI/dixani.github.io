FAQ

Business Tools — Frequently Asked Questions

Are DIXANI business calculators free?

Yes. DIXANI's online calculators are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

What business calculators are planned?

The first planned tools include a Profit Margin Calculator, Markup Calculator and Working Hours Calculator.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin measures profit as a percentage of selling price, while markup measures the amount added to cost as a percentage of cost.

Will DIXANI provide business templates too?

Yes. DIXANI already provides free operational templates for inventory, warehouse and purchasing activities, with more business templates planned.

Do I need business software to use these tools?

No. The tools are intended for straightforward browser-based calculations without requiring specialist business software.
