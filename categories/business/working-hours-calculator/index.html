FAQ

Working Hours Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate working hours?

Calculate the time between your start and end time, then subtract any unpaid break time.

Does the calculator support overnight shifts?

Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator treats the end time as occurring on the following day.

How are decimal working hours calculated?

Worked minutes are divided by 60. For example, 8 hours 30 minutes equals 8.50 decimal hours.

Are unpaid breaks included in working hours?

No. Any break entered in the unpaid-break field is deducted from the total shift duration.

Can I calculate estimated earnings?

Yes. Enter an optional hourly rate and the calculator will multiply it by your calculated decimal working hours.
